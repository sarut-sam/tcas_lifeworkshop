Four Rounds

Round 1

Portfolio

Portfolio, documents and often an interview. Open since 15 Aug. Each university and program sets its own window. Results 10–11 Mar. See the Round 1 Planner.

Round 2

Quota

University-run quota admission. Criteria and dates are set by each program.

Round 3

Admission

Central ranking with GPAX + TGAT/TPAT/A-Level. Apply 7–11 May.

Round 4

Direct Admission

University-run direct selection. 29 May–15 Jun.

The Exams

TGAT: general skills test. Most routes need it. TPAT 1–5: professional aptitude by field. TPAT1 = medicine. A-Level: subject exams. Subjects needed depend on the program.

Thai program

  • Based on GPAX plus TGAT / TPAT / A-Level results
  • Each program sets its own minimum GPAX and exam weights
  • Round 3 ranks applicants centrally on those scores
  • Check: the program page on myTCAS for subjects and weights

International program

  • Usually runs its own application, with its own deadlines
  • All the programs we reviewed in 2026 asked for an English test
  • Some also want a math test. Others weigh portfolio and interview more
  • Check: the university's own admission page and score bars

TAKE-HOME  ·  SAT

My SAT Planner

Mainly for International programs. Thai programs usually count GPAX, TGAT/TPAT and A-Level instead.
Tips for this step
  • First find out whether your target program asks for SAT at all. Many accept other tests (IELTS, TOEFL, CU-AAT) as an option.
  • Work backward: the score must reach the university before ITS deadline, not just before the test date.
  • The cut-offs below come from 2026 (TCAS69) program pages. TCAS70 numbers may differ, so confirm on the program page.

What the SAT Is

  • A digital test with two parts: Math and Reading & Writing (called EBRW in many program pages).
  • Each part is scored 200–800, so the total is 400–1600.
  • Programs ask for it in different ways: a Math minimum, a Reading & Writing minimum, or a combined total.
  • Always check which one your program uses.

TAKE-HOME  ·  ROUND 1

My Round 1 Planner

Round 1 is the portfolio round. Each university and program sets its own window and rules.
Tips for this step
  • Round 1 windows are different for every program. Some have already closed and some open in January, so check each one.
  • Round 1 judges what you have DONE (documents, projects, activities) and often an interview. It is not only about exam scores.
  • Check the myTCAS rules on confirming a seat before you confirm one.

Round 1 at a Glance

WhenWhatFrom today
Example of why you must check each program: Kasetsart's international economics program ran Round 1 track 1.1 from 18 Sep to 14 Oct 2026 (closed) and opens track 1.2 from 4 Jan to 4 Feb 2027 (interviews in English). Source: that program's TCAS70 page.

What a Round 1 Portfolio Usually Includes

  • Personal profile and education, with transcript (and GPAX)
  • English scores, and test scores where required (for example SAT, TGAT, UCAT)
  • Skills, projects or research, activities, awards and certificates
  • A short statement of purpose; some programs also want a recommendation
  • Often an interview, sometimes in English
In our 2026 data, many programs asked for a portfolio of up to about 10 A4 pages in Thai or English. Format and page limits differ by program. Use TCASfolio and TCAS Verified as your portfolio tools.
